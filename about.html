Dreams can come true...

Trish always enjoyed baking, but, at best, it was the occasional enjoyable treat for the family. As the experiments with new recipes grew more elaborate, Trish's treats became more and more popular among friends and family.

"You should open a business and sell these. They are amazing!" friends kept on saying, but Trish always chalked up their compliments as just being polite. Trish had no idea just how amazing her gourmet concoctions were.

Finally, as time progressed, and with enough encouragement, Trish tried her hand at going pro, and now finally all of Eastern Ontario can enjoy some sweet sweet Trish's Treats.

So come join us in Alexandria, Ontario for some of the best cupcakes that have ever existed. See you there!
